Connect your AI

Works with the AI you already use.

Prompter speaks MCP, the open standard AI apps use to connect to tools. You'll find your connector link in Prompter under the library or Appearance.

  2. In Claude, open Settings › Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it Prompter, paste the link and save.
  4. In any chat, ask for a script and say “put it on my prompter.”

Questions

Good to know.

Is it private?

Each prompter has its own secret key, and only someone with that key can see or send scripts to it. The connector link and device link contain your key, so keep them private like a password.

Do I need an account?

No. Your prompter is remembered in your browser. To use it on another device, open Prompter's library and copy the link for another device.

Which browsers work?

Everything works in modern browsers. Voice scrolling needs Chrome, Edge or Safari. Choosing a specific microphone works in Chrome and Edge.

What if I lose my link?

There's no account to recover, so save your device link somewhere safe. If you lose it, you can create a new prompter in one click, but the old scripts stay with the old key.

Does it record me?

No. Prompter only listens while voice mode is on, using your browser's built-in speech recognition to know where you are in the script. It doesn't store any audio.

Can I paste a script myself?

Yes. Press New in the library, paste or write your script, and you're ready to go. No AI needed.
